NodeMCU ESP8266

STANDBY

Microcontroller & WiFi

Primary processing unit responsible for gathering sensor telemetry, executing threshold logic, and transmitting REST packets.

Processor: Tensilica L106
Clock: 80 / 160 MHz
Wireless: WiFi 802.11 b/g/n
PIN D4 (GPIO2)

DHT11 Temperature Sensor

STANDBY

Ambient Thermal Probe

Precision digital temperature sensor measuring ambient thermal variations to detect heat anomalies and fire outbreaks.

Temp Range: 0–50°C (±2°C)
Protocol: Single-Bus Digital
Supply: 3.3V – 5.0V DC
PIN A0 (ADC)

MQ-2 Gas & Smoke

STANDBY

Combustion Gas Sensor

Chemical SnO2 sensor sensitive to smoke, LPG, propane, and hydrogen, detecting smoldering forest debris before flame breakout.

Range: 300–10000 ppm
Output: 0–1024 ADC
Supply: 5.0V DC
PIN D2 (GPIO4)

IR Flame Sensor

STANDBY

Infrared Optical Detector

High-speed photodiode with LM393 comparator tuned to infrared wavelengths emitted by active flames, ensuring millisecond-level detection.

Spectrum: 760–1100 nm
Angle: 60 Degrees
Comparator: LM393 Diff
